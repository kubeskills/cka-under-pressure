CKA Under Pressure: A Better Way to Practice

Chad Crowell · KubeCon NA 2026 tip talk

Don’t practice until the exercise feels familiar. Practice until you can solve it without the walkthrough.

The loop

ReadSay the requested outcome in one sentence. List constraints: names, ports, counts, and what must not change.
OrientConfirm context and namespace. Inspect what exists before you change anything.
SolveMake the smallest justified change. One change, then look.
VerifyTest each acceptance criterion by its behavior, from where the client lives.
ReviewName where you hesitated and why. Pick the one skill to drill next.

Preflight checklist

  • Right cluster and context? kubectl config current-context
  • Right namespace on every command?
  • Criteria read twice, constraints underlined?
  • Current state inspected (get, describe, events)?
  • I know what “done” looks like and how I’ll test it?
  • Mode chosen: learning, independent, or timed?

Remember

  • A successful command is not proof of a completed task. service/web exposed means the API accepted the object, not that traffic reaches your app.
  • Learn first, then add time pressure. The timer is a diagnostic tool for finding gaps, not the goal of every session. Fast typing is not competence.
  • Hints are data. Log which one you needed; it shows you the gap.

The ladder

  1. Learn the skill, no timer
  2. Attempt it on your own
  3. Verify against the criteria
  4. Review mistakes and hesitation
  5. Repeat with a variation
  6. Add the timer once the workflow is solid
